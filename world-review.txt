WORLD REFACTOR - OPEN QUESTIONS
===============================
Deliberately NOT fixed. Each is a judgement call about the world, not a
mechanical error. world.json currently reflects the CURRENT state described
under each item, so nothing here is lost - it is just possibly wrong.

Not a .md file on purpose: ServeDMD would publish it as a wiki page.


A. WRONG EDGES
--------------

A1. Nyzzrix - wrong on three counts
    now:  nyzzrix --range--> the-inkblot-bindery
    His page says he dwells in "the Emberveil Depths", far beneath the
    Heartforge. The bindery link looks thematic (memories, books), not spatial.
    He is also an individual, not a species, so `range` is the wrong relation
    (he got it because I tagged him `wyrmbalm, creature` and `creature`
    routes to range).
    likely: nyzzrix --found_at--> heartforge   ... or Emberveil Depths needs
            its own place node, with Nyzzrix found_at that.

A2. The Uroxi ranges into a city, not a region
    now:  uroxi --range--> umberdeep        (umberdeep is a city)
    Every other species ranges across a region. Should this be asara-plate,
    the region Umberdeep sits in? Or do the Uroxi genuinely live only in
    that one city?

A3. The Deity concept page has a location
    now:  deity --manifests_in--> the-fractured-keep
    `deity` is mechanic/deity.md, a "what is a deity" explainer, not a being.
    A concept does not manifest anywhere. Recommend dropping this edge; the
    page can list deities by tag query instead.
    This is the last survivor of the old is_a chain - the other 7 deities
    pointed at this page purely to say "I am a deity", and those were dropped.

A4. The Threefold Bloom: event or ongoing state?
    now:  threefold-bloom --happened_at--> the-dense
    Tagged `anchoring`. If it is a standing condition rather than a moment,
    `manifests_in` fits better than `happened_at`.


B. A RELATION SPLIT I AM NOT CONFIDENT IN
-----------------------------------------

B1. practiced_at vs originates_in
    I split professions by the SCALE of their target, not by meaning:
      practiced_at  (specific places)   harthrun-metallurgy -> heartforge
                                        scroll-binder       -> mohinders
                                        tankard-trekkers    -> city-of-or
      originates_in (regions/worlds)    dreamweaving        -> whispering-woods
                                        emberglass-artisan  -> duskreach
                                        tuskbreakers        -> skein-of-bones
                                        concordium-medica   -> iridia
    That is a size distinction wearing a meaning distinction's clothes. If
    Dreamweaving is still practiced in the Whispering Woods today, then
    `originates_in` is simply the wrong word for it.
    Options: (a) collapse both into practiced_at
             (b) keep both, re-sort by whether the craft ORIGINATED there or
                 is PRACTICED there now - some will need both edges
             (c) practiced_at + originates_in + still_practiced_in

G. STRUCTURAL, FOR LATER
------------------------

G1. Species range is single-valued today. Every species has exactly one
    `range` edge because the old schema allowed one parent. Real ranges
    probably span several regions - the schema now permits that, the data
    does not use it yet.

G2. Three realms exist: the-anchored, the-dense, the-fractured-keep.
    Only the first two are roots of the location tree. the-fractured-keep
    contains nothing at all (its only edge is A3 above, which should go).

G3. `scale` is doing two jobs: a tag on regions (armour-of-or is
    "region, scale") and a standalone concept page at
    location/landmark/scale.md titled "The Scales". The concept page is
    filed under location/ but is classified `concept`, not `place`.

G4. calendar.md already dates events in ARC in prose - Or Flatstone's dig at
    ARC 58, first public Iridian manifestation at ARC 51, Council of Elders
    at ARC 86. Those are `since` values waiting to be extracted onto edges
    and event nodes. The epoch block in world.json is ready for them.

G5. Rollup depth is unlimited, and it shows on Iridia.
    Relations declaring "rollup": "location" surface their subject on EVERY
    ancestor place, not just the immediate one. That is what makes Brother
    Ienor (found_at the Red Palm Hall) appear correctly under The City of Or.
    But it also carries all the way up: Iridia's page now lists 14 Residents,
    7 Native species, 3 drinks For sale and 5 Kept here - every drink in
    every tavern in the world.
    Fine for a 164-node world; will not stay fine. Options when it stops
    being useful: cap the depth per relation ("rollup_depth": 2), or roll up
    only to the nearest ancestor of a given kind - which is what the old
    RelationBuilder.In(targetTags) did before it was superseded.
