2a. gorgrath-the-brute BORN IN skein-of-bones
    "He was born in the Skein of Bones"
    He is now found_at city-of-or. Birthplace is a different fact from
    current location, and the vocabulary has no word for it. Add `born_in`?
    It would also fit Or Flatstone ("Born in poverty in a post-apocalyptic
    world") and would pair naturally with {since} once you date them.

2b. dottie-copperlocks <--?--> the-oblique-concord
    Deliberately ambiguous in the source, so I did not guess:
    "It is difficult to say whether Dottie fears the Oblique Concord, or
     admires them. Perhaps both. She never claims to oppose them."
    "She might be their creation. She might be their counterweight."
    "When asked if she serves them, she often laughs: I'd know... unless I
     wouldn't."
    Candidates: knows, member_of, rival_of - or nothing, if the uncertainty
    is the point. An edge with a {note} could record the ambiguity itself.

2c. drip <--?--> the-stable-flask
    "His relationship with **Professor Marwa Krindlenut** of *The Stable
     Flask* is both cordial and competitive."
    The relation is really Drip <-> Marwa, but Marwa has no page (see 3).
    rival_of the-stable-flask would be a stand-in for a person-to-person tie.

2d. ashenfang vs blooming-skein
    "The unchecked expansion of the Blooming Skein threatens to bury the
     Skein of Bones under living roots."
    A faction in conflict with a *region*. rival_of is declared for beings
    and groups; pointing it at a place may or may not be what you want.

2e. runehollow-hall and or-flatstone
    "Built by dwarves in honour of Or Flatstone himself"
    That is dedication, not authorship - so created_by would be wrong.
    Worth its own relation (dedicated_to) if this recurs.

4. WHAT WAS NOT MINED
---------------------
The scan found 718 pairs where one page mentions another with no edge
between them. Most are ordinary prose reference, not structure - the City of
Or is named on dozens of pages without being related to them. Only mentions
sitting in a sentence with a relationship cue (created by, friend, mentor,
member, rival, worships, dwells in...) were promoted to candidates, which
narrowed 718 to 84, and reading those left the 19 in section 1.

The remaining gap worth attention: calendar.md dates roughly 20 events in ARC
in prose. Those are `since` values for edges and event nodes, and extracting
them is the obvious next pass - it would turn the epoch block in world.json
from a declaration into a working timeline.
